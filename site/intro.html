paper-boxing is a small site manager for a home lab: a place where a project's documents live during early design and specification, before its first release, and where anything private lives permanently. Designed HTML pages, visual explorations and specifications are uploaded by a person or by an agent, served at a stable address on the LAN, and replaced in place as they change.

This site is the documentation of the newest release. The northstar states what paper-boxing is for; the architecture describes the four containers and how a person and an agent reach the one backend; the API contract, the deployment guide and the record of decisions are the Markdown documents, read on GitHub at the release's tag.
